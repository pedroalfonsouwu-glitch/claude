  - generic: Resumen
  - paragraph: Hard-won methodological rules governing evidence, verification, and version lineage — enforced after errors were caught
  - generic: Detalles
  - list:
    - listitem: "Numeric verification: nearest-number matching is prohibited — verification requires same species + property + magnitude + unit/valid conversion + context, simultaneously"
    - listitem: "Page-exact evidence: bibliographic entries must be page-exact, never range-expanded"
    - listitem: Multi-page range expansion was the source of 42 of 84 false positives identified in earlier versions
    - listitem: "Directness and coverage are independent axes in the master decision table: DIRECT/CORROBORATIVE/INFERENTIAL_FRAMEWORK is separate from FULL_ITEM/PARTIAL_ITEM"
    - listitem: "ZERO-LOSS lineage: all claims must maintain unbroken lineage across versions"
    - listitem: Rebuilding from scratch (as occurred in V4) rather than rebasing is a prohibited regression
    - listitem: The 11 NOT_APPLICABLE items must be preserved across all versions without exception
    - listitem: The Morrison/Boyd Study Guide is always supplementary and never elevates to primary source status
    - listitem: Figures derive from closed CSVs only — intermediate computation states must never be reported as final figures
    - listitem: Autocheck REPORT_LEDGER_NUMERIC_MISMATCH enforces the closed-CSV figures rule
    - listitem: Items without bibliographic backing are flagged NO_SUPPORT rather than carried without evidence — e.g. C38, C63, C83-A1b, C87-A1b, C68-A2b
